About the Club

Science, creativity, innovation and teamwork beyond the classroom.

SRCC Science Club is a science-based student organization of Shaheed Ramiz Uddin Cantonment College, established in 2015. The club aims to encourage scientific thinking, creativity, innovation, teamwork, and practical learning among students. Through workshops, presentations, science-related activities, and collaborative events, the club provides students with opportunities to explore science beyond the classroom.
