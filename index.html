About Us

Building a Culture of Science

SRCC SCIENCE CLUB is a student-driven community dedicated to developing curiosity, creativity and scientific thinking.

Our goal is to create an environment where students can learn, experiment, collaborate and transform ideas into meaningful projects.

Our Motto: SRCC SCIENCE CLUB — NEVER GIVE UP.
