About Us

Building a Culture of Science

SRCC SCIENCE CLUB is a student organization of Shaheed Ramiz Uddin Cantonment College, established in 2015.

The club aims to encourage scientific curiosity, creativity, innovation and practical learning among students. Through workshops, presentations, projects, competitions and collaborative activities, students get opportunities to explore science beyond the classroom.

We believe in learning together, sharing ideas and continuing to work toward improvement even when things become difficult.

Our Motto: NEVER GIVE UP
