About Us

Building a Culture of Science

SRCC SCIENCE CLUB is an official science community of Shaheed Ramiz Uddin Cantonment College, founded in 2015 with a vision to ignite scientific passion among young minds.

Our goal is to transcend classroom learning through hands-on practical experience, innovation, competitive problem-solving, and scientific research. We empower students to showcase their intellect and skills at national and international science arenas.

Guided by our motto "NEVER GIVE UP", we strive to build a strong community of future scientists, engineers, and visionaries.
